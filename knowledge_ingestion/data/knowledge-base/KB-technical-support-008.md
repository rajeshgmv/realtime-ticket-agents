## Ticket Description

Dear Customer Support Team,\n\nI am reaching out to request help with a problem I am facing on my PC. Recently, the audio hardware is no longer being recognized by the system. ## Resolution

Thank you for reaching out to us, <name>. If you have a removable audio card, reseat it if possible. If these steps help, please let us know the specific model of your audio device, whether it is built-in or external, so we can provide more targeted assistance.
