## Subject

Multiple Device Connection Problems

## Ticket Description

Dear Customer Support,\n\nWe are experiencing extensive connectivity problems impacting numerous devices throughout the office. The issues have been observed with headsets, printers, and workstations all at once, significantly disrupting daily activities. ## Resolution

Thank you for providing details about the connectivity problems affecting various devices. To assist you further, could you please specify whether the network outage affects both wired and wireless connections, and if any error messages are displayed on the devices? Also, kindly inform us of any recent modifications to your network configuration or infrastructure. If possible, please share relevant network logs or screenshots. We will prioritize your case and, if necessary, arrange a call at your convenience to accelerate the troubleshooting process.
